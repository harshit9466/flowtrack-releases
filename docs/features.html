Optional AI

AI Assistant

Generate end-of-day summaries, morning plans, and task descriptions from real session data. Your choice of six providers — from the Claude and OpenAI APIs to a local Ollama model if you'd rather nothing leave your machine.

Context-aware

Smart Nudges

FlowTrack notices when you return from idle, when you're working without a timer, and when a meeting starts — and offers the right action at that moment. Every nudge type can be turned off.
