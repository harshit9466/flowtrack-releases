Changelog

What's new, improved, and fixed in each release.

v2.39.0 October 10, 2026
🆕 New
  • The "No timer running" popup now finds your tasks. The box that says what you were doing also searches them, and with nothing typed it lists the latest ones. Pick one and the time goes onto that task — with the same "Backdate timer to…" as before — instead of onto a second task with a similar name. Typing something that isn't a task still starts a new one, and the popup tells you so before you press Start
  • Resuming a task, in the popup or in Quick Capture, has a box for notes about the session. It is optional: pick the task and press Enter to start, or write what you already know first. The note shows on the task page, goes into the Jira worklog comment, and is already in the note box when you stop the timer
  • The ticket page shows the Jira fields you chose in Settings → Jira → New ticket form, plus what Jira requires — Priority, Story Points, Team, Sprint, Labels and the rest — read from Jira when the page opens. Edit changes them, and Save to Jira sends only what you changed. A required field can't be emptied; if Jira refuses a value you see Jira's own reason with the field's name; a multi-line field that has formatting (a list, a link) is shown, and edited in Jira so nothing is lost
🔧 Improved
  • The "No timer running" window is taller, and its task list scrolls inside it while the question and buttons stay in place
  • Resuming a task from Quick Capture is now a click and Enter instead of a single click, so there is a place for a note. Ask FlowTrack and other AI apps can pass the note along when they continue a task
  • The ticket page no longer says priority, sprint or story points twice when the fields already show them
v2.38.0 October 5, 2026
🆕 New
  • Logging a session that had a break now asks what Jira should get: the time it counts (0:48), or that plus the break (1:09). A short break that was really part of the task — a quick meeting at a colleague's desk — can go into the worklog; a coffee break doesn't have to. Your own total in FlowTrack is the same either way, and time you chose not to count ("Don't count this time") is never added
  • The question appears where you log: under the row when you press Log hours, on the task page, right after you stop a timer that had a break, and in Ask FlowTrack's Log to Jira button. "Don't ask again" makes your answer the default
  • Settings → Jira → "When a session had a break": ask me each time, leave it out (what every worklog did until now), or add it
🔧 Improved
  • A worklog that went in with its break says so: the row's pill reads "Logged 1:09" instead of "Logged", and the task page says "incl. break" with a Change link. A worklog that went in without it offers "Add the break", in case you decide afterwards that it was part of the task
  • Until you answer, a session with a break is not sent to Jira on its own and is not sent without its break: it waits, and its pill reads "Log hours"
  • The Reports CSV has a column, Break added in Jira (min), so Jira's hours can be reconciled with Duration; Ask FlowTrack and other AI apps see how much break a worklog holds, and ask you before choosing for you
v2.37.1 October 5, 2026
🐛 Fixed
  • The numbers line and its filters (Type, Ticket, Jira hours) fit on one row again on a 1920-wide screen at 150 % scaling. The Breaks option added in 2.37.0 pushed the filters onto a second line there; the spacing between them is tighter now
v2.37.0 October 5, 2026
🆕 New
  • A break is now said where it matters. A session with a break inside it shows what was taken off under its time — 0:48 / 11:48 AM – 12:57 PM / +0:21 break = 1:09 — so the number beside the span is no longer a puzzle. The same sum is on the task page (under each session, and "21m break not counted" in the header), on the running timer, in Quick Capture and in the tray menu
  • A running timer's clock now stands still through its break, so it reads what will be logged when you stop (it used to climb through the break and then drop)
  • Edit session says what it will count, live: "This session will count 0:54 +0:15 break = 1:09" moves as you change the times, the break or the minutes not counted
  • A Breaks option in the Type filter shows only your break sessions — in the Today list, All Tasks, Reports and the AI apps. Breaks still show under All, and the day line says how long they took ("0:41 on breaks")
🔧 Improved
  • Time you chose not to count ("Don't count this time") is part of the same sum instead of a "−45m away" tag, so a session says all of what was taken off in one place
  • The Reports CSV has two new columns, Break taken off (min) and Away taken off (min), so End − Start can be reconciled with Duration
  • Ask FlowTrack and other AI apps see how long a session's break and time away were
🐛 Fixed
  • The tray menu and tooltip no longer show a different time from the dashboard for a timer that has a break
  • "Don't count this time" can't take off more than what still counts once a break is already off the timer
v2.36.0 October 5, 2026
🆕 New
  • Choose what a new Jira ticket asks, in Settings → Jira → New ticket form. The fields come from your own Jira for the project and issue type; what Jira requires is always on the form, and anything else is yours to choose. Each field can start with a value — story points, complexity, team, "the sprint that is running" — so you stop typing the same things. Until you choose, the form offers Team, Sprint and Original estimate as before
  • Create a Jira ticket from Quick Capture: "Also create a Jira ticket" sits beside "This is a meeting". It starts from your settings, says in one line what the ticket will be created with, and asks only for what Jira requires and nothing you saved covers. The ticket is made after the timer starts, so if Jira says no the timer keeps running and you can try again
  • Write with AI and Polish under every description box — the new-ticket description, Quick Capture, a session's Description, and the task page's description editor. Both are always there: write it from the summary, or tidy what you wrote. The new-ticket one also draws on your own session notes. Undo brings your words back
  • Renaming a task renames its Jira ticket too, and the sessions whose text was just the old title. Jira is asked first: if it says no, nothing changes and the page says why. A worklog already in Jira whose comment would change is marked out of date for you to update
🔧 Improved
  • The new-ticket form has real multi-selects (Project Phase, Components), labels, text areas and the estimate. The fields it can't fill (Assignee, a two-level choice like Service, Parent) are listed with the reason, and a project that requires one says so
  • Quick Capture's recent tasks are work only — meetings no longer take their places (searching still finds a meeting by name)
  • When Jira refuses a ticket you read its reason ("Project Phase is required."), not raw JSON
🐛 Fixed
  • Creating a ticket no longer ends with "A resource with the same unique identifier already exists" although the ticket was made
  • The Sprint choice on the new-ticket form loads again (it failed with "scope does not match"), and Team and Sprint say why when Jira refuses instead of showing an empty list. Nothing to reconnect

v2.30.0 September 29, 2026
🆕 New
  • Meetings are detected from your microphone. When Teams, Zoom, Webex, Slack, or a Meet, Teams or Zoom page in your browser uses the microphone for a minute, a meeting timer starts; it stops when the call lets the microphone go. The start and end are the exact moments Windows recorded, so the meeting is right even if FlowTrack notices late or restarts in the middle. Nothing to set up in Teams
  • Settings → Meeting Detection shows what it sees: the meeting going on now, or the last one it recorded
🔧 Improved

  • A meeting timer you started yourself is followed and stopped when the call ends, instead of a second one being started
  • Timers paused for a meeting stop at the moment the meeting began, so that time isn't counted twice
  • Meetings are named after the meeting, not after Teams' own windows like "Sharing control bar"
  • Other apps that use the microphone never start a meeting: OBS recording the call, Discord, dictation tools
🐛 Fixed
  • Teams meetings were not being detected at all. Detection depended on a Teams setting and a connection FlowTrack lost every time it restarted
